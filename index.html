A Legacy of Excellence

ABOUT VMC

Vidyamandir Classes (VMC) was established in 1986 by Mr. Brij Mohan (Bade Bhaiya) and his brother, Mr. Shyam Mohan (Chote Bhaiya). Their vision in founding this institution was to inspire, guide, and propel students toward realizing their ambitions in the fields of Engineering and Medicine.

Later, they were joined by Mr. Manmohan (Munna Bhaiya) and Mr. Sandeep Mehta (Sandeep Bhaiya). Today, this visionary quartet of IITians from Delhi has established Vidyamandir Classes (VMC) as the most ethical, honest, and preferred institute in the JEE / NEET landscape.

Over the years, fuelled by the guidance of its Founders and a crew of highly skilled & competitive faculty members, VMC has empowered thousands of students to excel in the highly competitive arenas of JEE & NEET.

VMC continues to redefine educational benchmarks, providing a platform where aspirations meet achievement through a blend of innovation, unique teaching methodology, holistic approach, expertise, and a relentless pursuit of academic excellence.

Vidyamandir Classes (VMC) thrives on a "Student First" ethos, enabling continuous success through a focus on concept-based learning rather than rote memorization. We nurture a genuine love for Science and Mathematics by teaching from the fundamentals, allowing students to grasp concepts deeply instead of simply memorizing formulas. What truly sets VMC apart is the active involvement of our founders, who remain dedicated to teaching and guiding students. Their passion for fostering excellence and making a meaningful difference in students' lives has made VMC a beacon of educational success for over 40 years, committed to handholding each student towards their goals.
